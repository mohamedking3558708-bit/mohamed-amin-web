Frequently Asked Questions

How many revisions are included?

Three revisions are included in every project so we can reach the result you want.

Can I request more than one video in a single project?

Absolutely. For larger quantities, I offer a special package with a better price per video.

How does payment work?

After we agree on the project, a deposit is paid upfront and the remainder is paid upon final delivery.

Do you accept urgent projects?

Yes, depending on my current schedule. A small rush fee may apply.

Can I review a draft before final delivery?

Yes, I'll send you a version to review before finalizing the project.

What should I prepare before we start?

The raw materials (video, audio, images), and any references or examples you like.

In what format and quality will I receive the video?

Any quality up to 4K, in a format suitable for the platform where you will publish it (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, or TV).

What payment methods do you accept?

PayPal - Binance - Insta Pay - Vodafone Cash - Western Union
